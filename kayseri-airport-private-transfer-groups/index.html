For 8 or more passengers, the published €110 Sprinter price is lower than €15 multiplied by the same passenger count. Cost is not the only factor, but this is the point where the private vehicle can also become cheaper than the shared total.

Luggage can change the practical capacity

The passenger limits above are maximum seat capacities, not a promise that every possible combination of oversized luggage will fit. Standard suitcases are normally fine. If the group has extra, oversized or special luggage, add it to the booking notes or contact WhatsApp before booking so the team can confirm the selected vehicle is suitable.

Same hotel or different hotels?

A private vehicle is designed for one booking. If the group is staying at the same property, the journey is straightforward. If passengers are split between different hotels, send the hotel details before booking so the route can be confirmed rather than assuming multiple hotel stops are included in one private price.

When Vito is the better group choice

Vito is the private option for up to 5 passengers. It is most useful when a family or small group values a direct journey, wants to avoid shared hotel stops, has a tighter schedule or prefers to keep luggage and passengers together.

When Sprinter is the better group choice

Sprinter is available for up to 16 passengers. It is the practical choice for larger groups and becomes especially attractive when the shared-shuttle total approaches or exceeds the fixed €110 vehicle price.

Round-trip planning for groups

You can arrange arrival and return in the same booking flow. For the return, the hotel pickup time is calculated from the departure flight and expected road time to Kayseri Airport, then confirmed in advance.
