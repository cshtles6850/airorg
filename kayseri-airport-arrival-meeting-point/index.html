Kayseri Airport (ASR) Arrival Guide

Kayseri Airport Arrival Meeting Point

A step-by-step guide for passengers who already have a Kayseri Airport to Cappadocia transfer booking: baggage claim, terminal exit, the name sign, what to do if you do not immediately see the driver, and how delays are handled.

Service information reviewed by Ekwo Travel · Updated 18 September 2026

ASR is Kayseri Airport’s IATA code. Travellers searching for an “ASR Airport to Cappadocia” transfer are referring to the same Kayseri Airport arrival point used for the service on this site.

1. Leave the aircraft and follow the terminal flow

After landing at Kayseri Airport, follow the normal arrivals route inside the terminal. Keep your phone available in case the transfer team needs to contact you about the booking.

2. Collect your checked baggage

If you checked luggage, collect it before going to the terminal exit. The driver meeting happens after you have completed the normal arrival process and are ready to leave the terminal.

3. Go to the airport exit

After baggage claim, continue to the public exit area. The driver waits at the Kayseri Airport exit with a paper sign showing the lead passenger name from the reservation.

4. Look for the lead passenger name

The sign is based on the lead passenger details in the booking, so the name entered in the reservation should match the person the group expects to see on the sign.

If you cannot find the driver

Stay near the terminal exit rather than moving to a different pickup area. Contact the transfer team on WhatsApp at +90 545 932 00 50 and send the lead passenger name plus the flight code. This gives the team the fastest way to match your message to the correct transfer.

If the flight lands late

We monitor the flight code entered in the reservation. If that aircraft lands late, the driver meeting follows the updated arrival and there is no extra waiting charge caused by the flight delay.

If baggage claim or the terminal exit takes unusually long

Flight tracking shows the aircraft arrival, but it cannot show whether your baggage is delayed or whether you are held inside the terminal. If you know you will be much later than other passengers from the same flight, send a WhatsApp message so the transfer team knows you are still inside.

After the meeting

The driver continues to the confirmed Cappadocia hotel. Shared service can include other hotel stops. Private transfer travels directly for the single booking. For cave or hillside hotels, the final access depends on whether the transfer vehicle can safely reach the property.

Fastest help if you do not see the driver: Stay at the terminal exit and WhatsApp the lead passenger name + flight code to +90 545 932 00 50.
