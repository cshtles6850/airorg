Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you book a Kayseri Airport transfer.

How much is the shared shuttle?

The shared shuttle is €15 per passenger on all six Kayseri Airport routes shown on this website.

How much are the private transfers?

Private Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers. Private Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers. These are vehicle prices, not per-person prices.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Enter the correct flight code in your booking. We monitor the flight using that code, and if the aircraft lands late we wait for the updated arrival without an additional waiting charge. If the flight code changes, send the new information to the transfer team.

What happens if my flight is completely cancelled?

Your transfer can be moved to a new flight. Send the replacement flight code and travel date to the transfer team so the reservation can be updated.

How do I find the driver at Kayseri Airport?

After leaving the terminal, look for our driver at the airport exit. The driver holds a paper sign with the passenger name written on it.

When does the shared shuttle leave the airport?

Shared shuttle service is organised by arriving flight. Once the passengers booked on that flight have arrived and met the driver, the vehicle leaves for the confirmed hotels.

How is my hotel pickup time decided for the return transfer?

We calculate the collection time from your departure flight and normal road time, then send you the confirmed hotel pickup time.

Is there an extra charge for night or early-morning transfers?

No. There is no additional night or early-morning surcharge. The same standard route price applies.

How do I pay?

Payment is made in cash to the driver. Euro, US dollars and Turkish lira are accepted. Card payment is not available.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel free of charge at least 24 hours before the transfer. Contact the transfer team as soon as possible if your travel plans change.

Why do you ask for passport numbers?

Passenger identity information is required for the reservation and airport transfer reporting process in Türkiye. Passport details are collected in the passenger-details step of the booking form, not in the first route-selection step.

Do arrival transfers take me directly to my hotel?

Yes. The arrival transfer is organised for the hotel or accommodation on your confirmed reservation. Return transfers collect you from that same confirmed property.
