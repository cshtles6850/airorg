Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you book a Kayseri Airport transfer.

How much is the shared shuttle?

The shared shuttle price is €15 per passenger on all six Kayseri Airport routes shown on this site.

How much is a private Vito?

Private Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers.

How much is a private Sprinter?

Private Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers.

Do arrival transfers take me to my hotel?

Yes. Arrival transfers are organised from Kayseri Airport directly to the hotel on your confirmed reservation.

Can I book a hotel-to-airport return transfer?

Yes. Return transfers collect you from your confirmed hotel and travel to Kayseri Airport.

Which Cappadocia areas are available?

Göreme, Ürgüp, Uçhisar, Avanos, Çavuşin and Ortahisar are listed on this website.

What booking information is required?

Date, flight information, hotel, passenger count, passenger names and passport numbers are entered on the booking page.

How do I receive confirmation?

Your completed booking request is sent to our team on WhatsApp for confirmation.

What happens if my flight is delayed or the time changes?

Send the updated flight information to the transfer team as soon as possible. The team will confirm the revised arrangement for your booking.

Are child seats available?

Child-seat availability and any related charge are not published yet. Please confirm this with the transfer team before booking if you need one.

Is there an extra charge for night or early-morning transfers?

No separate night-transfer policy is published on this website. Confirm the final booking details with the transfer team before travel.

What are the luggage rules?

Standard luggage can be entered in the booking notes. Rules for excess or special luggage are not published yet, so confirm these with the transfer team when relevant.

What is the cancellation policy?

The final free-cancellation window is not published yet. Confirm the cancellation conditions with the transfer team before travel.

How does payment work?

Payment details are confirmed by the transfer team with your booking. The website does not currently collect online payment.
