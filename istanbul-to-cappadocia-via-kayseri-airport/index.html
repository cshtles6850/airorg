How to Get from Istanbul to Cappadocia via Kayseri Airport

This site does not operate a road transfer from Istanbul. The practical journey is to fly from Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) to Kayseri Airport (ASR), then continue from ASR to your Cappadocia hotel.

Service information reviewed by Ekwo Travel · Updated 18 September 2026

The journey in four steps

  1. Choose a suitable flight from Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) to Kayseri Airport (ASR).
  2. Book the Kayseri Airport transfer using the correct ASR flight code and your full hotel name.
  3. After landing, collect your baggage and continue to the terminal exit.
  4. Meet the driver using the lead-passenger name sign, then continue to the confirmed Cappadocia hotel.

Which Istanbul airport should you use?

Use the Istanbul airport that gives you the flight schedule and fare that work best for your trip. This website does not sell flights and does not guarantee that a particular IST or SAW service operates on a specific date. Check the airline schedule before booking the ground transfer.

For travellers planning the Kayseri to Cappadocia, Turkey part of the journey after landing, this site covers the ground transfer from Kayseri Airport (ASR) to the confirmed Cappadocia hotel.

Why Kayseri Airport can still be a practical choice

Kayseri Airport can involve a longer road journey than the closest Cappadocia airport option, but airport choice is not only about driving distance. A more convenient flight time, fare or connection can make ASR the better overall itinerary for a particular traveller.

What happens after landing at ASR?

The airport-transfer process starts after you land at Kayseri Airport. Collect checked baggage, go to the terminal exit and look for the lead passenger name on the driver’s paper sign. If the booked flight is delayed, we follow that flight code and wait for the updated arrival without an extra flight-delay waiting charge.

Shared shuttle or private transfer after the flight?

Shared shuttle is €15 per person and may include several hotel stops. Private Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers; Private Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers. Private transfer is direct for one booking and is usually more convenient for families, groups, tighter schedules or more luggage.

How long is ASR to your hotel?

The normal planning range depends on the destination: Göreme 70–80 minutes, Ürgüp 70–80, Uçhisar 75–85, Avanos 60–70, Çavuşin 65–75 and Ortahisar 75–85. Shared hotel stops, traffic, weather and the exact property location can extend the journey.

Return to Istanbul

For the return trip, book the hotel-to-Kayseri Airport direction on the same destination route. We calculate the hotel collection time from your ASR departure flight and expected road conditions, then send the confirmed pickup time before travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide a road transfer from Istanbul to Cappadocia?

No. This website provides the ground transfer from Kayseri Airport (ASR) to Cappadocia hotels. It does not operate a road transfer from Istanbul.

Can I fly from either IST or SAW to Kayseri Airport?

Flight schedules vary by date. Check current airline availability from Istanbul Airport (IST) or Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) to Kayseri Airport (ASR), then book the ASR ground transfer separately.

What happens if my Istanbul to Kayseri flight is delayed?

Enter the correct ASR flight code in the transfer booking. If that booked flight lands late, we follow the updated arrival and wait without adding a flight-delay waiting charge.

How much is the transfer after I land at Kayseri Airport?

Shared shuttle is €15 per person. Private Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers and Private Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers.

Can I arrange the return from Cappadocia to Kayseri Airport?

Yes. Use the hotel-to-airport direction for the same destination. The hotel collection time is calculated from the ASR departure flight and expected road conditions.
