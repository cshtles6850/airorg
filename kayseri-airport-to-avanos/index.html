Avanos is a route where the first booking question is often the town itself. Accommodation platforms may use “Cappadocia” as a broad destination label, even when the property is actually in Göreme, Ürgüp, Uçhisar, Çavuşin or Ortahisar.

Plan the 70 km route from the real hotel town

Avanos is approximately 70 km from Kayseri Airport and the direct journey is normally around 60–70 minutes. That range can change with traffic, the exact property and shared hotel stops. It only applies when the accommodation is genuinely in Avanos.

Verify the town before you submit

Check the town name and full address on your hotel confirmation, not only the word “Cappadocia” on a booking platform. Selecting the correct town sends the transfer request to the right route and avoids trying to correct a destination after the flight has landed.

Shared or private for Avanos

Shared shuttle costs €15 per person and can include other confirmed hotel stops. It suits passengers who prioritise the lower per-person price. Private Vito or Sprinter is more direct for families and groups, and is useful when several passengers have luggage or the schedule matters more than the lowest total.

Flight tracking and meeting point

Enter the flight code, exact Avanos property and lead passenger details. The driver waits at the Kayseri Airport exit with the passenger name on a paper sign. If the booked aircraft is late, the meeting follows its updated landing time without a separate delay-waiting fee.

Return from Avanos to Kayseri Airport

For the return, we calculate hotel pickup from the departure flight and the expected road window to ASR. The confirmed time is sent before travel. Shared return service can include other hotel collections, while private service travels directly for one booking.

Avanos booking checklist

Before submitting, verify the town, full hotel name, address, flight code, passenger count and luggage. If the property is in another Cappadocia town, select that town’s route instead of booking Avanos because the platform uses a broad regional label.

Avanos Operational Notes

Town verification

The route depends on the real accommodation town. The full property name and address are more reliable than a broad “Cappadocia” label.

Hotel access

Service is arranged to the confirmed accommodation where vehicle access allows. If the final street is unsuitable, the nearest practical vehicle-access point is confirmed.

Shared journey

Shared service may include other hotel stops, so the total journey can exceed the direct 60–70 minute road range. Private service removes those additional stops.

Who should choose private

Private transfer is useful for families, larger parties, extra luggage or travellers with a tighter schedule. Return pickup is calculated from the departure flight.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm that my hotel is really in Avanos?

Check the town name and full address on the accommodation confirmation, not only the word Cappadocia. Some listings use Cappadocia broadly even when the hotel is elsewhere.

Why does the real town matter before booking?

The selected town determines the transfer plan. If the property is in another Cappadocia area, use that route instead of booking Avanos.

How long is Kayseri Airport to Avanos?

The direct journey is normally around 60–70 minutes when the hotel is in Avanos. Traffic, access and shared stops can change the total.

How does the shared shuttle leave Kayseri Airport?

It is organised around the arriving flight and leaves after the booked passengers have reached the meeting point.

Can I book Avanos to Kayseri Airport as a return?

Yes. We calculate the hotel collection time from the departure flight and confirm it before travel.

How do I pay?

Payment is cash to the driver in EUR, USD or TRY. Card payment is not available.
