Avanos should be booked only when the accommodation is actually in Avanos. Some hotel listings use the broad “Cappadocia” label even when the property is in another town, so checking the real hotel location avoids selecting the wrong route.

Kayseri Airport Arrival for Avanos

At the airport exit, the driver waits with the lead passenger name on a paper sign. We monitor the flight code entered in the reservation. If that flight lands late, we adjust to the actual arrival without adding a separate flight-delay waiting charge.

How the Shared Shuttle Departs

The Avanos shared shuttle is organised by arriving flight. It departs after the passengers booked on that flight have reached the meeting point, then continues to the confirmed accommodation stops. It does not operate like a public bus with a fixed hourly departure.

Check the Town Before Booking

Before submitting the reservation, compare the accommodation address with the town name. If the hotel is in Göreme, Uçhisar, Ürgüp, Çavuşin or Ortahisar, choose that route instead.

Private Transfer for Groups

For a family or larger party, a private vehicle keeps the group together from the airport to the same hotel. It is also useful when the party has more luggage and prefers not to make other hotel stops on a shared route.

Return from Avanos

We calculate the hotel collection time from the departure flight and expected road time, then send the confirmed pickup time. The return begins from the hotel on the reservation, with no separate night or early-morning surcharge.

Flight Changes

If the airline changes your flight code, send the new code to the transfer team. A completely cancelled flight can be moved to a replacement flight after the new details are confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the journey usually take?

The route is approximately 88 km and the planning range is around 75–90 minutes, depending on normal road conditions and the hotel location.

What if my flight code changes?

Send the replacement code to the transfer team so the reservation can be updated. We monitor the flight code attached to the booking.

How do I meet the driver?

The driver waits at the airport exit with the lead passenger name on a paper sign.

Why should I verify that the hotel is really in Avanos?

Some accommodation listings use the broad Cappadocia label. Selecting the actual hotel town avoids booking the wrong route.

Can I book a return to Kayseri Airport?

Yes. We calculate the hotel pickup from the departure flight and confirm the time before travel.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash to the driver in EUR, USD or TRY. Card payment is not available.
