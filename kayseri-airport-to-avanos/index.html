Approximately 70 km from Kayseri Airport

The Avanos route is approximately 70 km and usually takes around 60–70 minutes in normal road conditions. Traffic, shared hotel stops and the exact accommodation location can change the actual journey time.

Verify the town before you submit

Check the hotel address or confirmation page. If the property is actually in Göreme, Ürgüp, Uçhisar, Çavuşin or Ortahisar, select that route instead. This one step is more useful on the Avanos page than repeating generic transfer advice.

How the airport arrival works

The Avanos booking is linked to the flight code you enter. We monitor that arrival and arrange the meeting at the airport exit, where the driver shows the lead passenger name. If the same flight is late, the airport meeting shifts with it at no extra delay-waiting cost.

Shared service for Avanos

The shared shuttle is arranged around the arriving flight and leaves after the booked passengers have reached the meeting point. It can include other confirmed hotel stops, so it is best for travellers who value the per-person price more than a direct private journey.

Private service for groups

Families and larger parties often prefer a private vehicle because everyone travels together to one property and no additional hotel stops are added. It is also easier when the booking includes several pieces of luggage.

Avanos to Kayseri Airport

When travelling back from Avanos, the pickup is not left for the guest to estimate. We set it using the departure flight and the normal road window to Kayseri Airport, then confirm the hotel collection time. The standard fare also applies to very early or late pickups.

The Avanos check that prevents wrong bookings

Look at the actual town name on the accommodation confirmation, not only the word Cappadocia. If it says another town, use that town's route page instead of forcing the reservation into Avanos.

Avanos Operational Notes

Confirm the town

The first Avanos check is the real hotel town. Some accommodation platforms use the broad Cappadocia label, so the full property name and address help prevent an incorrect route selection.

Hotel access

Service is arranged to the confirmed accommodation where vehicle access allows. If the property cannot be reached directly by the transfer vehicle, the nearest practical vehicle-access point is confirmed.

Shared journey

Shared shuttle service can include other hotel stops, so the actual journey can be longer than the direct 60–70 minute planning range. A private vehicle removes those additional stops.

Who should choose private

Private transfer is useful for families, larger parties, travellers with more luggage or anyone working with a tighter schedule. Return pickup is calculated from the departure flight and expected road conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Avanos from Kayseri Airport?

The route is approximately 70 km and usually takes around 60–70 minutes in normal road conditions. Traffic, hotel location and shared stops can change the actual journey time.

Why should I verify that my hotel is really in Avanos?

Some accommodation platforms use Cappadocia as a broad location label. Checking the actual town prevents the wrong route from being selected.

How does the shared shuttle leave Kayseri Airport?

It is organised around the arriving flight and leaves after the booked passengers have reached the meeting point.

What if the airline changes my flight code?

Send the replacement code so the reservation can be updated and the correct arrival can be monitored.

Can I book Avanos to Kayseri Airport as a return?

Yes. We calculate the hotel collection time from the departure flight and confirm it before travel.

How do I pay?

Payment is cash to the driver in EUR, USD or TRY. Card payment is not available.
