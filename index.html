Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions travellers usually have before booking a Kayseri Airport transfer.

Is the shared shuttle €15 per person?

Yes. Shared shuttle pricing is €15 per passenger on the routes shown on this site.

Are private transfer prices per person?

No. Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers and Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers.

Can I book a return transfer to Kayseri Airport?

Yes. Return transfers collect you from your hotel and take you to Kayseri Airport according to the confirmed pickup time.

Do I need my hotel name when booking?

Yes. Your full accommodation name helps us organise the correct arrival or return transfer.
