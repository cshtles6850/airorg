Kayseri or Nevşehir Airport for Cappadocia?

A neutral planning comparison between Kayseri Airport (ASR) and Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport (NAV). This website provides transfers from Kayseri Airport only; it does not offer Nevşehir Airport transfers.

Service information reviewed by Ekwo Travel · Updated 18 September 2026

The main difference is location

Kayseri Airport is in Kayseri, while Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport is in the Nevşehir area. Both are official airports serving domestic and international traffic, but they approach Cappadocia from different directions.

For Göreme, NAV is geographically closer

From Kayseri Airport, this site uses an approximate road distance of 75 km to Göreme and a normal planning range of 70–80 minutes. Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport is geographically closer to central Cappadocia, so its road transfer to Göreme is generally shorter.

Which airport should you choose?

Do not choose an airport only by road-transfer time. Compare the flight schedule, airfare, arrival time and your final hotel. A shorter airport-to-hotel drive is not automatically the better overall trip if the flight option is less convenient for you.

Hotel destination still matters

Göreme, Ürgüp, Uçhisar, Avanos, Çavuşin and Ortahisar sit in different parts of Cappadocia, so the airport comparison changes slightly by hotel. If you are flying into Kayseri Airport, use the Kayseri Airport travel-time guide for the six destinations covered by this site.

What this site actually sells

kayseriairporttocappadocia.com only provides Kayseri Airport (ASR) transfer service. The purpose of this page is to help travellers understand the airport choice, not to suggest that Nevşehir Airport transfer is available here.

Why this comparison is intentionally neutral

We sell Kayseri Airport transfers, so this page does not pretend that ASR is always the better airport. Nevşehir Airport is geographically closer to many central Cappadocia areas. The correct choice depends on the flight that actually works for your itinerary.

Official airport references

Türkiye's State Airports Authority (DHMİ) lists Kayseri Airport with IATA code ASR and Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport with IATA code NAV. Both official airport pages are useful for checking airport identity and location before booking flights.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nevşehir Airport closer to central Cappadocia than Kayseri Airport?

Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport is geographically closer to many central Cappadocia areas. However, the better overall airport can still depend on flight schedules, airfare and your exact hotel.

Does this website offer Nevşehir Airport transfers?

No. kayseriairporttocappadocia.com provides Kayseri Airport (ASR) transfers only. A NAV arrival requires a different transfer provider.

How far is Kayseri Airport from Göreme?

This website uses approximately 75 km and a normal planning range of around 70–80 minutes for Kayseri Airport to Göreme.

Should I choose the closest airport or the best flight?

Compare both. Road distance matters, but a better flight time or fare can make the farther airport the more practical overall itinerary.
