Include Your Accommodation Details

Ortahisar accommodation is spread across different streets and parts of the town rather than one single hotel area.

Include the complete accommodation name when making the reservation. If the property has provided a location pin or vehicle-access note, keep that information available as well.

This is particularly useful for smaller cave and stone hotels.

Travelling as a Family or Group?

For several passengers travelling together, compare the total shuttle cost with the fixed private vehicle price.

  • Shared Shuttle: €15 per person

If you are travelling with a stroller, several large suitcases or unusual luggage, mention it when booking so the vehicle can be arranged accordingly.

Shared or Private?

The shared shuttle is the lower-cost option when you are comfortable travelling with other passengers.

Private transfer is better suited to families and groups who want their own vehicle and a more direct journey to the accommodation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the flight arrives later than planned?

We monitor the flight using the code provided in the booking. If the aircraft is delayed, we adjust to the actual landing time and wait without an additional flight-delay charge.

How do passengers meet the driver?

At the airport exit, the driver holds a paper sign with the passenger name. Make sure the lead passenger information in the reservation is accurate.

When does a shared Ortahisar shuttle leave the airport?

Shared departures are linked to the arriving flight. Once the passengers booked on that flight are present, the shuttle leaves for the confirmed hotels.

Do you collect me from my Ortahisar hotel for the return?

Yes. The return starts from the hotel on the reservation. We determine the pickup time from the flight departure and send you the confirmed time.

Is there a surcharge for an early-morning pickup?

No. The standard route price remains the same for night and early-morning transfers.

Can I pay in Turkish lira?

Yes. Cash payment can be made in Turkish lira, euro or US dollars. Card payment is not available.
