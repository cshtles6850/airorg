Tell Us Which Property You Are Staying At

Çavuşin is a smaller settlement, but accommodation is not concentrated in one identical location.

Cave and stone properties can be positioned around different parts of the village, so the transfer team needs the specific accommodation name, not only “Çavuşin”.

If your property has supplied a location pin or arrival instructions, keep them available with your booking details.

Staying in a Cave Hotel?

Smaller cave and stone hotels may have entrances that are less obvious from the main road than a large hotel complex.

The important point for your transfer is the exact property you are staying at. Include any special vehicle-access information supplied by the accommodation.

Travelling with Extra Luggage?

Passenger numbers and luggage volume are both relevant when arranging the vehicle.

If you are carrying several large bags, oversized luggage or travelling as a larger group, mention this in advance.

Returning to Kayseri Airport?

Return transfers are available from Çavuşin to Kayseri Airport.

We collect you from your accommodation according to the confirmed pickup time for your flight.

Shared journeys can include other hotel pickups, while a private transfer is reserved for your party.

Provide the property name, passenger count, flight number and departure time when booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will you wait if my flight lands late?

If the flight code in your reservation is correct, we follow the flight and wait for the actual landing time without charging an additional delay waiting fee.

Where does the driver wait at Kayseri Airport?

The driver meets you at the airport exit holding a paper with the passenger name. This makes it easier to identify the correct transfer after leaving the terminal.

How does shared shuttle departure work for Çavuşin?

The shuttle is organised around each arriving flight. It leaves once the passengers booked on that flight have reached the meeting point.

Why do you need the exact Çavuşin property name?

Çavuşin has accommodation in different parts of the village, including cave and stone properties with different road access. The exact name helps the driver reach the correct place.

How do you decide my return pickup time?

We use the departure flight time and expected road journey to calculate the hotel collection time, then communicate the confirmed time to you.

Is cash the only payment method?

Yes. Payment is made to the driver in cash. Euro, US dollars and Turkish lira are accepted; card payment is not available.
